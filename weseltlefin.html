Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Effective date: 29 September 2026  ·  Version 1.0.0

Short version: this app works fully offline. There is no account, no server, and no internet connection. Everything you write stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

About the App

وصلت لفين؟ ("Where did I leave off?") is a personal memory app. You use it to record where you stopped in something you are consuming or studying elsewhere: a book, a series, a course, a game, a podcast, an audiobook, manga, or your own study material.

The app does not host, stream, sell, or provide any content. It does not open Netflix, YouTube, or any other service. It only remembers the position you told it about, and the notes you wrote yourself.

The app never generates summaries, plot descriptions, chapter recaps, or any other description of a book, show, game, or course. Every piece of text you see inside the app was written by you.

Data Collection

What the app collects

The app does not collect information about you. It has no registration, no sign-up, and no user identifier. All content you enter is stored on your device, in its private application storage, and is visible only to you.

What you enter yourself

The following data is stored locally on your device, only when you type or choose it:

  • The name of something you are tracking, its type (book, series, course, game, and so on), and an optional date you started.
  • Your current position: a page, a season and episode, a lesson, a chapter, a mission, or your own custom labels.
  • An optional factual total, such as a page count or a lesson count, which you may enter to see a factual ratio such as 18 / 42.
  • Your own written notes: a memory note at a pause point, a general note, a "what to do first when I come back" line, and short "remember this" bullets.
  • An optional reason you stopped, chosen from a fixed list or written by you.
  • Optional photos you pick: a cover image for an item, or a photo of a page or your own handwritten notes.
  • Optional session records: the time you started and stopped a session, if you choose to log them.
  • Optional reminder settings: a date and time you choose for a local notification.

What the app does not collect

The app does not collect, request, or read:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials.
  • Your location, contacts, photos library contents, or browsing history.
  • Your viewing, reading, or listening history from any other app or service.
  • Any media library or playback data from your device.
  • Advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or advertising profiles.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it actually needs, and only at the moment you use the related feature:

Camera Used only if you choose to take a photo for a cover or a checkpoint. The photo is copied into the app's private storage and is never uploaded.
Photo library Used only if you choose to pick an existing image. The selected file is copied into the app's private storage.
Notifications Used only if you explicitly set a reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, microphone, or storage-broad access. If you decline a permission, every other feature of the app continues to work.

Use of Data

Your data is used for exactly one purpose: to display it back to you on your own device.

Specifically, the app uses your entries to show your continue shelf, your current position, your journey history, your own notes, and your optional reminders. There is no other use. In particular:

  • No data is used for advertising.
  • No data is used for profiling, analytics, or market research.
  • No data is used to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model.
  • No data is shared with any third party, because there is no third party to share it with.

Data Sharing

The app does not share your data with anyone.

There are no third-party analytics services, no advertising networks, no social integrations, no cloud storage providers, and no external content or metadata APIs connected to the app. The app is fully functional in airplane mode, which is the clearest demonstration that your data cannot leave your device.

The only way data leaves your device is if you, the device owner, deliberately export or copy it yourself, for example by taking a screenshot or by making a device backup through your operating system.

We are not in a position to access, view, correct, export, or delete your data, because your data is never transmitted to us.

Ads and Third-Party Services

The app contains no advertising. It does not display ads, sponsored content, interstitials, or affiliate links.

The app contains no third-party services of any kind. It does not integrate with social networks, streaming platforms, online book databases, game databases, course platforms, or any remote content service. It does not fetch cover images or summaries from the internet.

Because there are no ads and no third-party services, no advertising identifier, cookie, or tracking identifier is used anywhere in the app.

Data Security

Your data is stored in the app's private local storage on your device, using a local database. It is protected by the security of your own device: its screen lock, its operating system sandboxing, and its full-disk encryption if you have enabled it.

Because the app never transmits your data, it is never exposed to a network breach, a server compromise, or an accidental public leak.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you remove it. You can delete it at any time from inside the app:

  • Delete the history of finished items from Settings.
  • Delete an individual item, which also removes its checkpoints, notes, memory bullets, secondary bookmarks, and stored images.
  • Reset the entire app from Settings, which removes all stored data.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes its private storage.

There is no server-side copy and no cloud backup. If you delete your data, it is gone permanently and we cannot restore it for you.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no chat, no user-generated content visible to others, and no advertising. A parent may still choose to install the app for a child, in which case all the same rules apply: everything stays on that device.
