Zaraati (زرعتي)

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

100% Offline · No Account · No Data Collection

Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short version Zaraati is a fully offline farm management app. It does not require an account, it has no servers, and it never transmits anything you enter. Everything you record — land, crops, irrigation, expenses and journal notes — stays in the private storage of your own device.

Part One — Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what Zaraati does with your information. Because the application is built to operate entirely offline, the answer is deliberately simple: the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and shares nothing.

By installing or using Zaraati you accept the practices described on this page. If you do not accept them, please uninstall the application and delete its data from your device.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Zaraati does not ask for, and does not store, your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, identifiers, or any other personal information. The app is not linked to any account or user profile.

The only information the app handles is what you choose to record yourself, namely:

  • Farm details you enter, such as the farm name and the farmer's name.
  • Field records: field names, areas, and the units you selected.
  • Crop cycles: crop name, variety, planting date, expected harvest date and notes.
  • Activity records: irrigation, planting, fertilising, spraying, pruning, harvest and inspection entries with optional costs.
  • Expense records: title, amount, category, date and notes.
  • Journal entries: titles, notes, dates and any photos you choose to attach.

All of the above is written to the app's private storage area on your device. It is never transmitted off the device.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

Zaraati contains no tracking, profiling or advertising technology of any kind. Specifically, the app does not:

  • Collect your name, email address, phone number or any contact list.
  • Collect your precise or coarse location, and it never requests location permission.
  • Create advertising identifiers or device fingerprints.
  • Embed analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or advertising SDKs.
  • Read, scan or index the content of your device, other than the photos you explicitly select.
  • Request microphone, contacts, SMS, phone-state or call-log permissions.

4. How Your Data Is Stored

Records are kept in a local database inside the application sandbox, which is private to Zaraati on your device. Journal photos you attach are copied into the application's own documents folder under a generated file name.

Because storage is local and unencrypted at rest, anyone with physical or unlocked access to your device could read the data. Protect your device with a screen lock, and avoid leaving the app open on a shared device.

Backing up and transferring The app offers no cloud backup and no sync. If you uninstall the application, its data is removed with it. There is no automatic copy on any server, and therefore no way for us to recover anything for you.

5. Permissions the App Requests

PermissionWhy it is needed
Photo library access Used only when you tap attach photo while writing a journal entry, so you can select an existing picture to copy into the app.
Photos and media (Android) Same purpose as above, required by the Android photo picker for image selection.

No other permission is requested. In particular, the app never asks for internet access for its own operation, and it contains no networking code that would let it reach a server.

6. How Your Data Is Used

Your records are used for one purpose only: to calculate and display information for you inside the app, such as cultivated area, harvest countdowns, irrigation history, crop ages, expense totals and category breakdowns. This processing happens on your device.

Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, analytics or model training.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share data. There is nothing to share. Zaraati does not sell, rent, trade, license or otherwise disclose any information to third parties, because the app has no third parties and no channel through which data could leave your device.

We will not disclose your data in response to legal requests, because no copy of your data ever exists outside your device. If a device is lost, stolen or seized, the data on it is removed with it, and we will have no information about it and no ability to retrieve it.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained for as long as the application remains installed on your device.

  • Delete an individual record: use the delete action on the record, or swipe the item in the relevant list.
  • Delete a field: deleting a field also removes the crops, activities, irrigation records, expenses and notes linked to it, after your confirmation.
  • Delete everything: use Settings › Privacy and Data › Clear all data, which erases every record and returns the app to its first-launch state.
  • Uninstall the app: the app folder and all of its contents are deleted by the operating system.

9. Children's Privacy

Zaraati is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children, it does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has stored sensitive information in the app on a device you control, remove it with the delete actions above or uninstall the app.

10. Security

The app is designed to minimise risk by removing the need to move data: there is no login to steal, no session to hijack, and no server to compromise. Standard device protections apply. We recommend a screen lock, and keeping the app updated through the store you installed it from.

11. Changes to This Policy

If the policy changes, the updated version will be published with the application listing and the Last updated date at the top of this page will be changed. Because no data is collected, no notification to individual users is possible or required.
