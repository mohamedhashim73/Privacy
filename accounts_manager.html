Accounts Manager — مدير الحسابات

Privacy Policy & Terms & Conditions · Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Accounts Manager (مدير الحسابات) is an offline-first Flutter app for tracking income, expenses, client invoices, payments, and monthly financial reports. It is designed for small businesses, accountants, and shop owners. The app works fully offline: all records stay on your device using local storage (Hive and SharedPreferences). There are no accounts, no servers, and no cloud sync.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data you enter — transactions, clients, invoices, payments, and login preferences — is stored only on your device. We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, locations, contacts, device identifiers, analytics, or usage statistics.

Use of Data

Data stored on your device is used solely to provide app features: showing the dashboard, listing transactions and clients, recording payments, and generating monthly reports. The local login (email and password stored on-device) is used only to restrict access to the app on that device.

Data Sharing

We never share, sell, rent, or transmit your data to anyone. Since the app is offline-only, your data never leaves your device unless you uninstall the app, clear its data, or manually share your device.

Ads & Third-Party Services

The app contains no ads, no trackers, no cookies, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party services. It requests no internet permission for its core features.

Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). The in-app login adds a basic access barrier on the device but is not a cloud security system. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and for backing up any important records externally, as uninstalling the app permanently deletes local data.

Children's Privacy

This app is a business finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
