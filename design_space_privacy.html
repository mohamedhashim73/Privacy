OFFLINE APPLICATION

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Design Space (مساحة المصمم) — a fully offline workspace for UI/UX designers.

Effective date: 1 March 2026  ·  Applies to the Design Space mobile application for Android and iOS.

In short: Design Space works entirely on your device. It has no account server, no cloud storage, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you create stays on your phone unless you deliberately export or share it.

1. Information We Collect

Design Space does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details.
  • Your device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or IP address.
  • Your location, contacts, calendar, or call logs.
  • Usage analytics, session recordings, crash traces tied to you, or behavioral profiles.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following content only on your device, using the app's private local storage:

  • Sign-in state: whether the app's built-in local sign-in has been completed.
  • Projects: project names, client names, descriptions, notes, categories, progress, start and deadline dates, colours, and task lists.
  • Inspiration board: saved ideas, tags, notes, and images that you choose from your device's gallery or capture with the camera.
  • Colour palettes: palette names, notes, and colour values you create.
  • Profile and preferences: the optional designer profile you fill in, your light/dark/system appearance choice, and your language.

Because this data never leaves your device, we cannot access, view, recover, export, or delete it. You are the only controller of it.

2. How We Use Your Information

Because the app collects no information from you, there is nothing for us to use. All data is used solely on your device so that the app can:

  • Display the projects, tasks, inspirations, and palettes you created.
  • Remember your sign-in state, theme, and language between launches.
  • Show the images you selected from your own gallery.

Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, analytics, model training, or any other purpose.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any information with third parties. Since no data is transmitted off your device, there is nothing for us to disclose to advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, law enforcement, or any other party.

We will only disclose information if we are ever legally compelled to do so, and because we hold no user data, such a disclosure would not involve your content.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

  • The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • The app uses no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, or telemetry SDKs.
  • The app contains no trackers, cookies, or web views and makes no network requests at runtime.
  • The app requires no internet permission for any of its features to work.

The app's only third-party code is open-source software included in the distributed application package. It performs no network activity.

5. Data Security

All content is kept in the application sandbox, which the operating system protects from other apps. Data does leave your device in only two ways, both initiated by you:

  • You copy a colour's HEX code to the clipboard, which is readable by other apps until you overwrite it.
  • You export or share content through your own device, outside the app.

No transmission over the internet is used, so there is no encryption-in-transit requirement for the app's storage. As with any local application, a rooted or jailbroken device, or malware on your device, could access app data; please keep your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

6. Children's Privacy

Design Space is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

If you believe a minor has stored personal information in the app on your device, delete the app. All of its local data is removed with it.

7. Your Rights and Controls

Under privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA you have rights over personal data, but because we hold none, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You have full control directly on your device:

  • Edit or delete any project, task, inspiration, or palette inside the app.
  • Use the in-app “Reset all data” option to erase every project, inspiration, palette, and stored image, then restore the sample content.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its data permanently from your device.

8. Deleting Your Data

To delete everything the app has stored:

  • Open Profile → Data & Privacy → Reset all data. This clears every project, task, inspiration, colour palette, and locally stored image, then re-adds the demo content.
  • Or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device.
